# Report Template

Use this exact layout for every report. Keep the section order and headings.
Write in plain, short sentences. Avoid legal terms; if one is needed, explain
it in brackets the first time, for example "indemnify (pay for the other
side's losses)".

---

## Template

```markdown
# Fine Print Report: [Document type]

**Pages read:** [N] of [N] · **Clean PDF:** [file name]
**Overall:** [N] High · [N] Medium · [N] Low

## Summary

[Under 150 words. Say what the document is, who it is between, and what the
user is agreeing to. End with the one or two things that matter most.]

## Key facts

- **Parties:** [who is on each side]
- **What you must do:** [main duties of the user]
- **What they must do:** [main duties of the other side]
- **Money:** [amounts, fees, deposits, interest, when payments are due]
- **Dates:** [start, end, renewal, deadlines, notice periods]
- **How to leave:** [how to end the agreement, notice needed, any cost]

Write "Not stated" for any fact the document does not give.

## Flags

| Risk | What it says (exact quote) | What it means for you | Question to ask |
|---|---|---|---|
| High | "[exact quote]" (p. [N]) | [plain explanation] | [question] |
| Medium | "[exact quote]" (p. [N]) | [plain explanation] | [question] |
| Low | "[exact quote]" (p. [N]) | [plain explanation] | [question] |

[Order rows High, then Medium, then Low. Within a level, order by page.
If there are no flags, replace the table with: "No red flags were found in
the pages read. This does not mean the document is safe to sign."]

## Unclear or unreadable parts

- [Page N, where on the page: what could not be read and why]

[If everything was readable, write: "All pages were readable."]

## Before you sign

- [ ] [Action tied to the highest flag, e.g. "Ask to cap the late fee in writing."]
- [ ] [Action tied to the next flag]
- [ ] Fill in or strike out any blank spaces.
- [ ] Keep a signed copy of every page.
- [ ] Get any promises made out loud added in writing.

## Important

This report is an automated reading aid, not legal advice. It may miss
things or get things wrong. Laws differ by country and state, and some
clauses flagged here may not be enforceable where you live, while others
may matter more. For high-value or high-risk documents (a home, a large
loan, a job with a non-compete, or anything with High flags you do not
understand), talk to a lawyer or a free legal aid service before signing.
```

---

## Filling-in rules

- **Summary under 150 words.** Count them. Cut detail, not the key risk.
- **Page numbers** refer to the order of the photos (photo 1 = page 1).
- **Quotes** must match the page text exactly. Put long quotes into the table
  with "..." in the middle, never paraphrase inside quotation marks.
- **Missing-item flags** (category 9) use *Not found in the document* in the
  quote column instead of a quote.
- **Before you sign** has one action for each High flag first, then the
  standard items. Keep it to 8 items or fewer.
- **Never** write "this document is safe", "you can sign this", or "this is
  standard so don't worry".
- Keep the disclaimer word for word.
